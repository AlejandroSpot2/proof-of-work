Who is the example builder?

Rin Okafor, a fictional builder. Every file, commit and employer in the example is synthetic, so you can try the whole flow with no keys.

Can the Studio read my own projects?

Yes. Live mode reads public GitHub repositories, read-only, and needs a model key on your machine.

Can I edit the portfolio's wording?

Yes. You edit the words in the Studio and the facts stay linked to their files. Change a fact and anything built on it is withdrawn.

Does a model write my portfolio?

In live mode a model drafts the statements from your files; the example uses recorded drafts. Every draft passes the quote check, and you review it in the Studio.

Is my portfolio published anywhere?

No. A portfolio link is read-only, you choose who gets it, and you can revoke it.

Is the kit legal advice?

No. It is a draft you prepare for an attorney to review, with no eligibility assessment.
