# The Studio: direction and idea

## The idea

Rin's own files become the portfolio's material. Each project cover is drawn from what its files show: Tidepool's offline merge as a timeline you can scrub, Quietroom's feedback turning into clusters and issues, Kerf's release artifacts with Rin's part lit and the deploy engine left with its author. Under every sentence sits its source, one click away.

The signature moment: in the Studio, the files just read turn into the portfolio as a swarm of cursors places each block. It is a composition illustration of a result already computed, labeled as such, skippable, and never shown as agents working live.

## Two outputs, one set of facts

1. **The portfolio**, published only by the owner as a revocable read-only link. Editorial, big covers, project detail with a shared-element transition, proof on demand.
2. **The attorney packet**, a typeset PDF for a first consult: cover, profile and objective, review summary, contributions with attribution, impact and limits, chronology, evidence index with exhibit IDs and page references, annexes with literal excerpts, missing corroboration, questions. Every fact comes from the same confirmed statements and source files as the portfolio.

## Art direction: paper and cobalt

- Ground `#f7f7f5` paper, cards `#ffffff`, ink `#0e1116`, muted `#565c68`, hairlines `#e1e1dc`.
- One accent, cobalt `#2747e8`: the primary action, selection, the proof control, the live part of a concept illustration. Nothing else is colored.
- One dark field, ink `#0e1116`, reserved for the attorney packet section, the "trust" moment.
- Type: Geist for everything a person reads; Geist Mono only for file names, exhibit IDs, commit hashes and dates (system-generated things).
- Scale: hero name at display size, project titles large, body 16-18px. App screens follow the app-screen rules; the published portfolio follows the page rules.
- Motion: one curve, `cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1)`. Clip-path reveals, the swarm, the shared-element zoom into a project, hover and press feedback. `prefers-reduced-motion` shows the final state.
- Concept art is drawn in HTML and SVG in the same palette and always labeled "Concept illustration". No fake screenshots of users or code.

## Words

People words only: "Rin's part", "Others' part", "what the files show", "proof". No ledger, claim, fixture or projection on a visible surface. EXAMPLE labels stay visible.
